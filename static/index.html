// HOW IT WORKS

From "where do I go" to "there" in three steps.

The order matters here — each step confirms the one before it, so nobody is routed to a resource that's already gone.

01 · LOCATE

Search your radius

Drop a pin or share your location to see every open resource within walking or driving distance.

02 · VERIFY

Check the last update

Each listing shows who confirmed it and when, so you know the shelter or clinic is still actually running.

03 · NAVIGATE

Get routed there

Turn-by-turn directions that avoid closed roads and known hazard zones reported by other users.
